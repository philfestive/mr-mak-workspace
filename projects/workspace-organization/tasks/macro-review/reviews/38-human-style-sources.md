## Authority and useful property clues

The [Arty board](C:/Dev/studio/arty/RACE-STYLE-AUTHORITY-BOARD.md) owns existing
working terminology. It distinguishes construction family, zone context,
portable variation, material role and evidence status. Nine base working styles
remain; the separate `bright_cartoon_dusk` test label is retired. Pastoral,
Frontier and Shadowed are proposed Human variants, not three additional approved
styles or registered runtime profiles. Existing Human-world direction does not
choose Human characters for the new product.

The [Human trial card](C:/Dev/studio/arty/work/trial-style-preparation-01/BRIEF.md:44)
proposes broad rounded masses, clean silhouettes, few meaningful practical pieces
and large material-role groups. Addressable handmade joins should support the
whole object rather than accent every seam. Its nine-subject envelopes are fair
study controls, not production dimensions. A crate's opening, containment and
frame/slat relationship matter more than a generic "clay" label.

Palette evidence has a separate home. The
[SHAR v2 card](C:/Dev/ResearchDump/_reference/_analysis/dataset_wave_r1/lanes/drop2_merge/SHAR_BRIGHT_PROPERTY_CARD_V2.md)
places ratified amendments above an inherited v1 body. That hierarchy matters:
the amendment changes accent-reserve scope/bands and expands hue accounting.
Useful mechanisms include tinted neutrals, warm lightness ladders, hue-preserving
darks and role-based chroma allocation. District dials vary independently. These
are franchise measurement findings, not swatches or numerical requirements
accepted for this project. Texture-space distributions and rendered-frame
coverage also measure different things.

Shadow and material clues should stay distinct. The
[territory doctrine](C:/Dev/ResearchDump/_reference/STYLE_TERRITORY_DOCTRINE.md)
records lower Human-dark values with chroma retained, rejecting noir
desaturation. SHAR research describes vertex prelight and shadow decals;
the cozy authored props instead show soft matte surfaces under a studio rig.
Neither route establishes one selected mobile shader. A material metaphor does
not settle geometry, shadow tint, outline policy or physical rendering cost.

## Questions, coverage and next owner

The next focused round should define the first **Human/cartoon subject**:
an everyday prop, building/street scene or human character, and its exact retained
reference. Then ask which shape/material properties matter: broad rounded masses
or straighter practical parts, chunky proportions, softened or visible facets,
matte painted surfaces, subtle grain or another finish? Finally ask whether
shading should use smooth volume, declared bands or authored flat value groups,
with what shade tint and outline treatment. Palette-role questions follow:
which surfaces receive stronger chroma and which keep quieter contrast? Save
these answers separately; Human/cartoon does not inherit SHAR v2's numerical
thresholds, and a neutral crate cannot answer the whole color philosophy.

Render-cost clues are incomplete. Nineteen source meshes and three material
roles do not establish three draw calls, affordable silhouettes or phone memory.
SHAR's measured prop geometry ranges describe its reference corpus; they are not
budgets for these Blender exports. [Current Arty reconciliation](C:/Dev/studio/arty/work/trial-style-preparation-01/RETURN.md#current-crateq66-reconciliation-and-lesson-landing--2026-10-11) still reports
crate-context captures undelivered and Q66 INCONCLUSIVE. No retained source here
proves sustained mobile 30 FPS.
