## 1. Purpose and experience

Player, platform, perspective, intended experience and design pillars.
Describe the actual selected game; examples and references are not decisions.

## 2. Core loop

Player action -> feedback -> consequence -> next choice.
Include objectives, progression, success/failure and a concrete example.

## 3. Selected scope

First playable slice, included features/content and explicit exclusions.
Keep later possibilities separate from the accepted implementation scope.

## 5. Content, world and progression

Selected levels, encounters, items, characters, progression and required data.
Identify source identity and any unresolved material/provenance questions.

## 6. Controls, camera and interface

Inputs, camera behavior, player information, interaction flow and accessibility.

## 7. Art, animation and audio

Selected direction, accepted references, constraints and criteria for review.
Distinguish a generated concept from a selected look or an integrated game asset.

## 8. Technical constraints

Engine/version, target devices, performance expectations, networking/save rules
and dependencies. Unknowns remain named open questions.
