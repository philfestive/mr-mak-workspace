## 2. Direction register

**Reported direction** attributes the retained decision to its source. **Retained vocabulary** records the research's own scope/status. **Working interpretation / prompt language** is this package's synthesis, not a new owner selection. **Scoped study** applies only where actual retained study evidence exists. ### Human — `bright_cartoon`

**Reported direction:** Q114 names Human `bright_cartoon`; Q118 includes it in the first trial-preparation group. The older board selects `SHAR-bright` specifically for Human territory/environment. A general token and environment evidence do not complete Human character-material design. [L1; L2; L4]

**Retained vocabulary:** Environment research uses strong facade masses, roof/body/support/opening hierarchy, broad painted regions and controlled detail. The older character sheet's soft torso taper, mitten-like hands and wedge boots are historical WORKING vocabulary. Its exact material remains separate. Western/Prairie Town descriptions are historical context, not the active Classic gameplay requirement. [L3 Human]

**Working interpretation / prompt language:** Clear cartoon masses, broad coherent color grouping and an understandable functional object. When discussing a prop, describe the structure and useful regions rather than importing character anatomy or franchise symbols.

**Source/Godot visual request:** Preserve the selected silhouette, function and important region relationships in the intended view. Do not require every physical part to have equal visual contrast.

**Open / do not infer:** Exact character finish, universal clay, every region maximally bright, a fixed palette, a shader or outline policy. The selected reference texture statistics remain specimen-specific. [02D–02J]

### Human / Duskwood — `bright_cartoon_dusk`

**Reported direction:** Q114 lists it as the Human/Duskwood variant, not a ninth race. The historical board's exact Human-dark definition includes “values lowered, chroma kept, Sly shadow layer; noir desaturation rejected.” This preserves a reference relationship without prescribing a mechanism. [L1; L2 glossary]

**Retained vocabulary:** A darker Human register retaining the Human construction/property system. The mention of a Sly shadow layer does not merge this register with Night Elf or select any specific conversion-kit ramp, outline or shader. [L2; L4]

**Working interpretation / prompt language:** Darker grouping of the selected Human construction while keeping the chosen color relationships present. Distinguish the painted regions, shadows and display treatment when their contributions matter.

**Source/Godot visual request:** Maintain the selected Human shape relationship and judgeable functional regions; do not replace the intended treatment with uniform grey desaturation.

**Open / do not infer:** A numerical RGB/HSV/HSL transform, exact lighting or palette, or an independent learned package versus Human-register conditioning. The historical chroma phrase is not a measured universal transform. [02E, 02J]

### Night Elf — `noir_graphic`

**Reported direction:** Q114/Q118 first trial group. The historical board records Sly-derived graphic direction for **characters and environments**. That selected scope remains; it does not make character-texture measurements environment measurements. Clay studies may inform form without mandating clay surfacing. [L1; L2; L3]

**Retained vocabulary:** Long continuous gestures, sweep and taper, deliberate negative spaces, controlled asymmetry and designed shadow/value regions. The October cards are reported preparation, not generated assets. [L3; L4; L5]

**Scoped study:** Marker-04 and C2 retain their separate study KEEP. C2 demonstrates a bounded construction contrast and also records side-depth/joining limitations; it does not prove all-subject recognition, environment rendering or production language. [L4]

**Working interpretation / prompt language:** Swept/tapered construction with the specific openness required by the function; coherent graphic light/dark regions and restrained subsidiary detail. Describe contour treatment separately.

**Source/Godot visual request:** Preserve the **selected opening or negative-space relationship**, relevant side thickness and complete silhouette. Do not require every object to have a central hole or the threshold's arch outline.

**Open / do not infer:** Outlines remain PARKED / owner-level conflict, not ON or OFF. Exact environment property coverage, palette, shading mechanism and full material response remain bounded by actual sources. External TF2/Gooch examples are not Night Elf rules. [02C, 02E, 02J, 02K]

### Dwarf — `bright_clay`

**Reported direction:** Q114 look-word trial direction. Older research uses pressed plasticine and rolled/flattened/pushed primary forms. A later label does not adopt every historical ratio, copper treatment or prop recipe. [L1; L2; L3]

**Retained vocabulary:** Broad formed masses, selected compression, full softened contours, substantial parts and meaningful joining. Character-only cheeks, beard coils and anatomy do not become universal prop components. [L3; L6]

**Scoped study:** Marker-04/C2 keep their own calibration/transfer scope. Shelf B KEEP preserves fullness, formed deck and bowed supports; its experiment omits local pressure motifs and changed deck AND support geometry. The join-read is closed at NO_JOIN_CHANGE_JUSTIFIED within retained evidence. [L4; L6; L7]

**Working interpretation / prompt language:** Substantial hand-formed masses, broad worked contours, calm surrounding surfaces and understandable support relationships. Localized pressure marks are chosen features, not mandatory identifiers.

**Source/Godot visual request:** Preserve the selected forming and whole-object relationships. A numerical separation is not automatically a visible flaw; convincing visible support is not physical-bearing proof.

**Open / do not infer:** A thumbprint everywhere, a ban on all hardware, a required squat portal, copper palette or universal matte parameter. Washer/button-like additions were failures in the marker question, not a general rule against every fastener. Broad support can be shared craft. No reopening B from this research. [02A, 02B, 02H]

### Troll — `dark_clay`

**Reported direction:** Q114 supplies the name; Q120 explicitly retains a Troll clay-scope question before a Troll studio. This pass does not answer that owner question. [L1]

**Retained vocabulary:** Long soft-rectangular torso rhythm, very long forearms, broad splayed feet, blunt tusks, relaxed stance and dry hand-smoothed clay. Sun-bleached/lilac-grey character language and later marsh proposals remain distinguishable. Neverhood is reference-only, not a selected identity. [L2; L3 Troll]

**Working interpretation / prompt language:** Elongated but substantial clay-like forms, broad extremities and restrained handling variation. For character research, preserve the relaxed massing rather than turning elongation into gauntness.

**Source/Godot visual request:** Carry the selected character or prop properties only within that named subject. Do not silently propagate the dark label into all territory lighting or every surface.

**Open / do not infer:** Whether dark means material, palette, mood or territory in the eventual selected application; literal black, wet swamp slime, horror, exact Neverhood copying, or the historical lilac palette as a new production choice. No Troll studio/run is selected here. [02A]

### Orc — `bold_toy`

**Reported direction:** Q114 supplies the trial name. Historical sources favor a cheerful matte toy/mascot read; glossy 1990s mascot CG is a separate proposal, not a settled replacement. [L1; L2; L3 Orc]

**Retained vocabulary:** Convex rounded slab chest, substantial forearms/hands, broad blunt masses and practical lash/plate relationships. Character anatomy is not a literal environment recipe. [L3]

**Working interpretation / prompt language:** Bold convex proportions and coherent substantial pieces. Matte painted finish is wording supported by the historical track, not the token's final finish decision. Use the named function before choosing a handle, support or body shape.

**Source/Godot visual request:** Preserve the selected mass hierarchy and joint readability; keep geometry, finish and light response separately attributable.

**Open / do not infer:** Glossy vinyl, cereal branding, photoreal musculature, spiked grimdark styling, studded-brick, or a universal no-specular material. Matte/satin/gloss research remains a real question rather than something the word toy resolves. [02A, 02B, 02J]

### Gnome — `turned_toy`

**Reported direction:** Q114; the later trial header and review explicitly keep Gnome brick-free. The historical atlas uses turned-part/wind-up vocabulary. [L1; L2; L5]

**Retained vocabulary:** Cylinders, discs, spheres/cones, clear local axes, profile changes, rings and deliberately fitted repetition. Creature medium between toy/clockwork-like and tidy natural-clay forms remains unresolved in the atlas. [L3 Gnome]

**Working interpretation / prompt language:** Ordered toy-like profiles and coherent assemblies. Beads, coves, shoulders and collars can be features of one part; they need not become a pile of independent attachments.

**Source/Godot visual request:** Preserve chosen local axes, profile hierarchy and fitted relationships. Independently replaceable components require an actual consumer/identity reason, not a glossary noun.

**Open / do not infer:** Studded-brick, mandatory gears, working clockwork, wood grain, glossy plastic, creature mechanism or race assignment for every round existing prop. Finish and real articulation remain separate. [02B–02D]

### Undead — `mended_ceramic`

**Reported direction:** Q114 names the trial; the board attributes D-UNDEAD-01 to the character register **repaired ceramic keeper**, anti-horror and **no green**. The constructed gothic puppet was considered and declined. [L1; L2]

**Retained vocabulary:** Thin rounded ceramic-doll masses, careful replacements and localized mends. Unglazed bisque, restrained craze and gold-like/contrasting repair details are historical WORKING or UNRESOLVED material language—not a final recipe. The atlas's environment, prop and creature entries remain separately labeled proposals. [L3 Undead]

**Working interpretation / prompt language:** Cared-for ceramic-like construction whose particular repairs communicate continuity and care rather than blanket decay or random crack decoration.

**Source/Godot visual request:** Preserve the selected repair's location and hierarchy in the complete object. A moving reflection does not necessarily move the mend itself.

**Open / do not infer:** Exact ceramic, palette, repair material, gloss or rig; compulsory gold, sewing, gore, green decay or the declined puppet identity. A glazed alternative mentioned in earlier package research was a **new exploratory suggestion**, not recovered owner selection or equal-status historical material. Kintsugi is not a synonym for every repair. [02A, 02H–02J]

### Tauren — `plush_clay`

**Reported direction:** Q114 token; the historical research explicitly uses plush as form reference, with clay masses and painted coat flow. Literal felt/plush production is not selected. [L1; L2; L3 Tauren]

**Retained vocabulary:** Full rounded barrel, gentle muzzle, blunt horns, broad soft extremities, calm weight and broad coat regions. Exact seams and the translation into environment/prop construction remain separate proposals. [L3]

**Working interpretation / prompt language:** Full gentle clay-like volumes with plush-informed proportions and broad coat organization. This is not a request for a teddy-bear costume or microscopic fiber detail.

**Source/Godot visual request:** Preserve the selected soft mass relationships and region placement. Appearance of softness does not prove squashing, fur behavior or a performance advantage.

**Open / do not infer:** Felt, stitching, strand/card/shell fur, a fur shader, subsurface values, deformation/rig or fuzzy architecture. Character form evidence cannot fill an unselected building specification. [02A, 02H, 02J]

### Goblin towns — `studded_brick`

**Reported direction:** Q114/Q119 identify the goblin-town look; Q118 includes it in the first group. The September board's undefined association is preserved as history, not repeated as the current answer. This is a place-directed trial, not a ninth playable race. [L1; L2; L5]

**Retained vocabulary:** Discrete studded construction-toy parts and planned assemblies. Brick Stunt Rally supplies a construction-system reference, not Bright Frontier dimensions or mechanics. Gnome remains a distinct brick-free direction. [L2; L5]

**Working interpretation / prompt language:** Blocks and larger bays forming understandable structures, with the selected stud pattern supporting rather than overwhelming that composition.

**Source/Godot visual request:** Preserve the chosen part grouping, functional opening and stud scale. Consumer resource sharing, collision, part interchange and runtime cost require their own evidence.

**Open / do not infer:** A toy brand's logos/dimensions, universal quarter-turn rules, grid gameplay, functioning snap sockets, literal masonry, playable Goblin assignment, automatic batching or a finished 27-asset set. [02C, 02D]

### Reference selection and comparison limits

A source should support the specific property and application scope being discussed. A screenshot, extracted texture, conversion-kit effect, creator breakdown and generated proposal are not interchangeable evidence. A useful character reference can remain useful without qualifying an environment rule. A source described as missing in a dated audit is not proven absent everywhere today, nor proven recovered by a later summary that never names it.

Use matched function/context when the comparison needs them, without forcing equal framing or neutral surfaces onto every question. C2's geometry-only diagnostic does not prohibit palette in a complete-style study. Dwarf B's scoped KEEP remains a preference for its combined contour changes, not isolation of the deck's contribution. A board crop can expose a detail while hiding whole-object context; return to the complete view for the overall claim. See [02K examples](../evidence/WAVE-02K-REFERENCE-AND-COMPARISON-EXAMPLES.md).

## 4. Prompts are applications of the interpretation, not a second style authority

Use the relevant wave's proposed prompt blocks as examples. Adapt only to a named subject and actual workflow. Record deviations from this library as proposals rather than silently changing the style through a prompt preset. An internal token is not a guaranteed model vocabulary item, and an avoid-list is not assumed to reach negative conditioning.

For source/Godot translation, state what the complete object must preserve, then let the owning technical lane bind actual mechanisms. Do not infer a shader, per-role mesh, new node hierarchy or physical bearing from descriptive language.

#### Primary public references consulted for terminology

**E1 — National Film Board of Canada, Animation Techniques: Stop-Motion (2018).** Distinguishes the technique and clay stop-motion. No fixed game frame rate is imported from this reference.
https://blog.nfb.ca/blog/2018/08/13/animation-stop-motion/

**E2 — Museum of Pop Culture / LAIKA, Hidden Worlds: ParaNorman.** Documents fabricated/printed replacement faces as a stop-motion puppet technique. It does not select a game rig or pipeline.
https://www.laikahiddenworlds.com/paranorman

**E3 — Victoria and Albert Museum, An A–Z of Ceramics, “B is for biscuit.”** Explains unglazed fired wares and why some porcelain is intentionally left unglazed.
https://www.vam.ac.uk/articles/a-z-of-ceramics

**E4 — Smithsonian Center for Folklife and Cultural Heritage, Japanese Kintsugi and the Art of Repair with Yuko Gunji.** Describes a specific repair practice involving lacquer and gold powder. It does not make gold mending mandatory for the Undead style.
https://folklife.si.edu/magazine/japanese-kintsugi-yuko-gunji

**E5 — Aardman Academy, Model Making Foundation.** Describes clay model-making and armature work. Used only as material/process terminology, not a course or purchase recommendation.
https://academy.aardman.com/model-making-foundation

**E6 — Aardman, Flushed Away.** Identifies the film as entirely CGI rather than stop-motion. Used to distinguish production medium from a studio/reference association.
https://www.aardman.com/film-tv-games/flushed-away/
