## How to refine an example

Keep exact source/run, property being discussed, inspected evidence/limits,
your reaction and next question. Say which visible aspect you mean: shape,
silhouette, color, shadow treatment, outlines, material or camera-distance
readability. A preferred property need not mean accepting the whole old project.
