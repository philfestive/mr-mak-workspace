# Clear goals for the Refiner

Current phase: define the philosophy and its specifics. Explore best retained
runs as examples; migration and product implementation come later.
The owner's direction and answers live in the
[design-direction source](../../projects/workspace-organization/design-direction.md).
The measures below are proposed ways to see progress, not new product rules.

## Four macro outcomes

| Goal | Useful result |
| --- | --- |
| M1 - Clear routes | Know where each project's design, sources, examples and outputs belong |
| M2 - Specific philosophy | Make mobile performance, art quality and toon colors concrete enough to judge a design |
| M3 - Coherent selected design | A chosen scope has a GDD, source index and linked schema contracts |
| M4 - Traceable change | Changes update their owning source and identify the affected outputs and evidence |

**M1 - Clear routes.** Distinguish prior 2D/3D sources from a future product and
from this presentation workspace. The visible result is a small source-linked
map, not copied repositories or another task board. Examples identify an exact
run and what it can demonstrate. Next owner: this organizing session; the shared
Home writer integrates its route. See [examples](examples.md) and the source
comparison in [Setup review](review.md).
Dependency: bounded exploration and a confirmed Home writer. Proposed exit:
the relevant source, answer and output routes resolve and their owners are clear.

**M2 - Specific philosophy.** Begin with minimum devices, sustained FPS,
representative scene workload and the exact preferred visual examples. Then
define shading, color, shapes, materials and scalable costs. Progress is explicit
answers with consequences and remaining unknowns, not a filled template or an
inherited old budget. The next questions are in the design-direction source.
Next owners: the human and the assisting Refiner session; subject reviewers
provide examples and challenge ambiguity.
Dependency: named example/style and concrete device/workload choices. Proposed
exit: the selected style has explicit visual decisions and measurable performance
intent, with remaining unknowns visible. The owner has chosen mid-range Android
first, then iPhone, and 30 FPS; 8+ styles are refined one at a time.

**M3 - Coherent selected design.** Once a product/slice is chosen, link its GDD,
decisions, requirements, schema contracts and planned checks from one project
entry. Existing adequate sources retain their homes. This phase is ready for
implementation when the selected scope is understandable and its material open
choices are resolved; no specific game or slice has been selected here.
Next owner: the eventual selected project writer. Current selection remains
with [Build focus](../2026-10-12_workspace-home/focus.md).
Dependency: owner selects a product/slice and its source home. Proposed exit:
the chosen scope's requirements, contracts and expected observations are linked
and understandable. Contracts identify stable IDs, field types/requiredness,
relationships, constraints/defaults, ownership/version and valid/invalid examples
where relevant. Outstanding questions that change this selected scope remain
visible; future possibilities stay separate. This is proportional to the chosen
slice, not a requirement to finish every future system before useful work.

**M4 - Traceable change.** When a source, answer, run or consumer changes, update
that owner first and mark affected claims for review. Preserve useful unchanged
evidence and retain old decisions as superseded. Progress is a real change
followed through its links, not another review of a review. See
[Drift plan](drift-plan.md). Existing project boards own current work.
Dependency: an actual changed answer/source and known dependent claims. Proposed
exit: the owning fact and affected routes are reconciled without invalidating
unaffected evidence. Next owner: the writer of the changed source; Setup2 maintains
this review's routes, while shared Home/registry receiving stays with its confirmed writer.

## How the other work contributes

- MCP T005 supplies tool/profile/version and qualification routes. It does not
  choose game design or certify a phone budget.
- Character Lab T006 owns its character/revision catalog and ongoing refinement.
  A catalog entry, selected look and target consumer have different evidence.
- Arty, GodotWorld3d, Audio, Shaders and Recompile retain their existing sources
  and writers. This workspace references useful examples and lessons.

Thirty-six explorers were launched in three waves of up to twelve. Their
[assignment and review record](../../projects/workspace-organization/tasks/macro-review/brief.md)
supports these outcomes; agent count and the research allowance are not design
completion. Cross-session receiving requests are in
[coordination](../../projects/workspace-organization/tasks/macro-review/coordination.md).
