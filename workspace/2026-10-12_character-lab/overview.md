# Character Lab

Turn character ideas into visible versions, refine the chosen direction, and
deliver an editable asset to a named project. Keep the design choices and their
results together so the next step starts from a known reference.

Idea -> versions -> refinement -> editable source -> named export and consumer
proof. Take the next step that serves the deliverable you need now.

## What the lab should produce

- A clear character idea and a visual direction you can explain.
- Reviewed versions with a recorded choice and a useful next question.
- An editable sprite, illustration or model and export, with a named destination and evidence from
  the project that uses it.

## Start with the next decision

Use [Characters](#/character-lab/1) to find a character and its versions. Use
[Pipeline](#/character-lab/3) to choose the next output and what will finish that
stage. [Styles](#/character-lab/4) explores 2D, 3D and Arty terminology;
[Directory](#/character-lab/5) shows project/subproject depth and source destinations.
[Lessons](#/character-lab/2) preserves useful findings from earlier work.

The first personal character brief remains to be chosen. Start with the idea,
its intended use, and the next design question. Name the deliverable before
producing its alternatives. Start from the
[brief template](../../projects/character-lab/templates/character-brief.md) and
[round template](../../projects/character-lab/templates/round.md).

## Keep a character's work together

One character card keeps its question rounds, references, prompts, comparisons
and choices together. Each round names its exact base, what to keep and change,
what the results actually show, and what comes next. Earlier takes remain
available when the direction changes.

Arachne is a separate bundled sample of earlier exploration. Its versions are
examples of a working process; they do not choose a personal character or a
current model destination for this lab.

Project rules, source layout, selections and technical checks live in
`projects/character-lab/README.md`. Workspace Home Tasks owns current assignments;
the existing project owners keep authority over their sources and consumers.
